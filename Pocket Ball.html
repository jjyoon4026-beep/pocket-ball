Tips for wall (cushion) shots

1. A ball bounces off a wall like light off a mirror: it leaves at the same angle it came in.
2. To bank a shot, aim at a spot on the wall, not at the ball.
3. Look at the dashed line. Each orange number is one wall touch.
4. A soft shot is easier to control. A strong shot goes farther and bounces more.
5. Three-ball: you need 3 wall touches before the 2nd ball. Four-ball: your last point needs 1 wall touch before the 2nd red ball.

Drag back from anywhere, then let go to shoot. Pull farther for more power.
